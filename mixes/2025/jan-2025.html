January 2025 Mixtape

2025-01-11
0:00

A very trappy and bass-heavy mix. This mixtape was recorded as an audition for a slot for SMU Stereometa's involvement at Gravical 2025, and I wanted to go for something a bit heavier and more energetic than my previous mixes.

I did get to play, but my slot turned out to be during a break where nobody was climbing. So I ended up playing something a bit more tame that day.
