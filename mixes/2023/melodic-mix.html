Melodic House/Techno Mixtape

2023-06-26
0:00

This is a mixtape that I put together as a demo tape for Singaporean DJ collective Revelry, who reached out to my university's disk-jockeying club looking to feature some DJs for a melodic techno night they were holding. However, I'd been told that this vibe wasn't quite what they were looking for, as it was too heavy on the melodic house and light on the techno.

I still really like this mix though, as it presents a fairly cohesive journey from gentler melodic house into the darker and more technical vibes of techno. With the mix being close to an hour long, I also had more time to let transitions play out, as well as present a mix that might be conducive for both active listening and background music.
