About

Project Prawn started out as a general-purpose creative project in 2020 that included photography. It has since come to focus on music since 2022, and this name was chosen because my surname sounds like the Chinese word for "prawn".

I mix electronic music that covers a diverse range of genres, but I have a particular fondness for melodic house and techno.

While I haven't had much experience in the club scene due to my niche preferences and chilled-out style, I've been fortunate to have had the opportunity to play at Cherry in 2023, as well as offering my services at Singapore Repertory Theatre and Gravical 2025.

Since then, I've opted to mix at home and for friends.
